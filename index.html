    Why I built it

    Every election night somebody screams fraud and somebody else screams landslide, and neither one checked the numbers. These are the state's own counts, updated daily, so you can watch the trend instead of the spin.

    What to watch

    Whether the Democratic edge in early ballots is bigger or smaller than it was at the end of 2022. In-person early voting is where Republicans close the gap, and more states open it every day through November 3.

    Where the numbers come from

    State election offices via the Election Data Guy tracker, cross-checked with Dr. Michael McDonald's UF Election Lab. The 2022 baseline is from the US Elections Project.
