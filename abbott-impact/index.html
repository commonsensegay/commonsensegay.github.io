Abbott Impact

Help keep Texas red, right from your phone.

Early voting in Texas starts Monday, October 19. Abbott Impact is the free volunteer app from the Texans for Greg Abbott campaign. It matches your contacts to the Texas voter file, so you can see which of your friends and family have already voted and remind the ones who haven't with one tap. Elections are won by whoever shows up, and nobody gets your friends to show up better than you.

Straight answers

Does it cost anything?

No. Signing up and using the app is free.

Do I have to live in Texas?

Sign-up asks for a Texas address, and the voter file it uses is the Texas voter file. If you're outside Texas, here are the apps for other states.

Will the voters I text see my phone number?

No. Texts and calls to voters go through a private campaign number. Your personal number stays hidden.

Do I need training?

No. The app gives you the script for texts, calls and doors.

Is this a government website?

No. Abbott Impact is a campaign app run and paid for by Texans for Greg Abbott. Common Sense Gay links to it because getting Republicans to vote early is how we win.

When does Texas early voting run?

Early voting starts Monday, October 19. Election Day is Tuesday, November 3. Check your county's dates and polling places.
