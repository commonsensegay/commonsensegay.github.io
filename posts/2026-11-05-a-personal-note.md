I'm working through some personal things, and I won't be posting on my social media accounts for now. But I'm not giving up. I'll be back in due time.

Let's be honest, I also need a break from the overwhelming negativity I've received.

The midterms were well fought by the Democrats, and I wish them success as they legislate these next two years. Now it's time to focus on the things the American people actually care about: affordability, healthcare costs and America First policies.

Those America First policies are what won Republicans the trifecta in 2024, and they didn't follow through on that mandate. This is now a second mandate to fix these systemic problems.

And I'll say this. In 2024 I didn't gloat. I didn't threaten the people on the left who thought they would win. Telling people to kill themselves isn't civil discourse, and it's not the conversation we should be having when we all care about this country. At least most of us do.

I look forward to the break, and I'll see y'all real soon.
